# Ethanol: alcohol, ancol, ancol etylic
Ethanol is an alcohol with molecular formula C2H6O and condensed structural formula CH3CH2OH. Its hydroxyl group is bonded to a saturated carbon atom. It is a primary alcohol. Ethanol mixes with water because its hydroxyl group can form hydrogen bonds. Complete combustion produces carbon dioxide and water: C2H5OH + 3 O2 -> 2 CO2 + 3 H2O. Ethanol can oxidize to ethanal and then ethanoic acid. Ancol ethanol có nhóm hydroxyl -OH. Công thức ethanol là C2H5OH. Ethanol thuộc alcohol bậc một.
