# Ethanal: aldehyde, andehit
Ethanal has condensed structural formula CH3CHO. Its aldehyde functional group is -CHO. The carbonyl carbon of an aldehyde is bonded to at least one hydrogen atom. Oxidation of ethanal can produce ethanoic acid. Reduction of ethanal can produce ethanol. Aldehyde khác ketone ở cách liên kết của carbonyl carbon. Ethanal là một aldehyde, có công thức CH3CHO.
