# Water: nước, hydrogen bonding
Water has molecular formula H2O. A water molecule has a bent shape and is polar. Water molecules can form hydrogen bonds with each other. Hydrogen bonding helps explain its relatively high boiling point compared with similar small molecules. In acid-base chemistry, water can donate or accept a proton. Nước có công thức H2O, phân tử có cấu trúc gấp khúc và có tính phân cực.
