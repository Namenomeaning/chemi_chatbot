# Ethene: alkene, etilen, anken
Ethene has molecular formula C2H4 and condensed structural formula CH2=CH2. It is the simplest alkene. Its carbon-carbon double bond includes one sigma bond and one pi bond. Ethene can undergo addition reactions. Hydrogenation of ethene produces ethane. Ethene can form polyethylene through addition polymerization. Etilen ethene là alkene đơn giản nhất, có công thức C2H4 và liên kết đôi carbon-carbon.
