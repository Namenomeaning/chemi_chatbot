# Ethanoic acid: carboxylic acid, axit axetic
Ethanoic acid, also called acetic acid, has condensed formula CH3COOH. Its carboxyl functional group is -COOH. It is a weak acid: it ionizes only partially in water. It reacts with bases to form salts and water. With ethanol under acid-catalyzed esterification, it forms ethyl ethanoate and water. The esterification is reversible. Axit axetic có tên IUPAC ethanoic acid. Nhóm chức carboxyl là -COOH. Ethanoic acid phản ứng với ethanol tạo ethyl ethanoate.
